## Insight 1: Sales Are Growing Year-Over-Year (Sales Trend)

**Observation from Dashboard:**  
The sales trend line chart shows total sales increased from ₹10.62 Crore in 2024 to ₹11.08 Crore in 2025 — a growth of approximately 4.3%.

**Data Evidence:**  
- 2024 Total Sales: ₹10,62,41,000 | Profit: ₹1,64,72,754  
- 2025 Total Sales: ₹11,07,76,600 | Profit: ₹1,68,33,558  
- Profit also grew by ~2.2% year-over-year.

**Business Interpretation:**  
The business is on a growth trajectory. Both revenue and profit are increasing, which indicates healthy business expansion. However, profit is growing at a slower rate than sales (2.2% vs 4.3%), which suggests rising costs or increasing discounts may be compressing margins.

**Recommended Action:**  
Investigate cost drivers and discount patterns in the months where sales grew but profit did not grow proportionally. Consider reviewing the pricing strategy for high-volume months.

## Insight 3: Technology Dominates Profit — Furniture Lags Behind (Category Profitability)

**Observation from Dashboard:**  
The category profitability chart shows Technology far exceeds other categories in profit generation, while Furniture generates relatively low profit despite significant sales.

**Data Evidence:**

**Business Interpretation:**

Review Furniture pricing and discount strategies urgently. Consider reducing heavy discounts on Furniture sub-categories like Tables and Bookcases which are likely driving down margins.

## Insight 5: High Discounts Destroy Profit — Orders with 31%+ Discount Run at a Loss (Discount Impact)

**Observation from Dashboard:**  
The discount vs profit scatter/bar chart shows a clear downward trend: as discount percentage increases, average profit per order falls sharply and turns negative at high discount levels.

**Data Evidence:**  
- 0% Discount: Avg Sales ₹64,210 | Avg Profit ₹13,203  
- 1–10% Discount: Avg Sales ₹58,834 | Avg Profit ₹10,010  
- 11–20% Discount: Avg Sales ₹46,490 | Avg Profit ₹6,336  
- 21–30% Discount: Avg Sales ₹39,523 | Avg Profit ₹3,181  
- 31%+ Discount: Avg Sales ₹32,349 | **Avg Profit: –₹1,601 (LOSS)**

**Business Interpretation:**  
Orders with discounts above 30% are actually costing the company money. This is a serious profitability risk. The deeper the discount, the lower the average order value, meaning customers are buying less even with deep discounts.

**Recommended Action:**  
Implement a maximum discount cap of 25% across all product categories. Any discount above this threshold should require manager approval with clear justification.

## Insight 6: Standard Class Shipping Has the Longest Delivery Time but Still Used Most (Shipping Performance)

**Observation from Dashboard:**  
The shipping performance view shows Standard Class has the longest average delivery time (4.7 days) but is the most commonly used shipping mode.

**Data Evidence:**  
- Same Day: Avg Delivery 0.4 days | Avg Sales ₹60,428  
- First Class: Avg Delivery 1.77 days | Avg Sales ₹50,712  
- Second Class: Avg Delivery 2.68 days | Avg Sales ₹53,333  
- Standard Class: Avg Delivery 4.71 days | Avg Sales ₹50,442

**Business Interpretation:**  
Most customers choose Standard Class (likely for cost reasons), but they experience the slowest delivery. However, Same Day shipping generates the highest average sales per order, suggesting customers who want fast delivery are also high-value buyers.

**Recommended Action:**  
Offer Same Day and First Class shipping promotions to Standard Class customers who place orders above a certain value threshold. This can increase revenue and improve customer satisfaction simultaneously.

## Insight 7: Furniture Has the Highest Return Rate — Especially Tables and Bookcases (Return Pattern)

**Observation from Dashboard:**  
The return analysis chart shows Furniture has a significantly higher return rate compared to other categories.

**Data Evidence:**  
- Furniture Return Rate: 7.67%  
- Office Supplies Return Rate: 3.65%  
- Technology Return Rate: 3.03%  
- Overall Return Rate: 4.55% across 4,200 orders

**Business Interpretation:**  
Furniture items — particularly sub-categories like Tables and Bookcases — are being returned at more than twice the rate of Technology products. High return rates signal potential product quality issues, inaccurate product descriptions, or customer expectation mismatches.

**Recommended Action:**  
Conduct a root cause analysis on Furniture returns. Add better product images, dimensions, and material descriptions to product listings. Consider a stricter return policy for bulky furniture items or offer assembly assistance to reduce dissatisfaction.

## Insight 8: Technology Sub-Categories (Copiers, Accessories, Phones) Are the Top Profit Drivers (Business Opportunity)

**Observation from Dashboard:**  
The sub-category profitability breakdown shows that the top 4 most profitable sub-categories are all Technology-related.

**Data Evidence:**  
- Copiers: Sales ₹4.06 Cr | Profit ₹73.1 Lakh  
- Accessories: Sales ₹3.92 Cr | Profit ₹71.9 Lakh  
- Phones: Sales ₹3.84 Cr | Profit ₹70.97 Lakh  
- Machines: Sales ₹3.57 Cr | Profit ₹64.5 Lakh  
- Binders (lowest): Sales ₹22 Lakh | Profit ₹3.26 Lakh

**Business Interpretation:**  
The Technology category is not just the largest revenue driver — it also delivers the highest profit margins at the sub-category level. Copiers and Accessories are particularly efficient profit generators. Meanwhile, low-value Office Supplies like Binders and Paper contribute minimally to overall profit.

**Recommended Action:**  
Increase inventory and marketing investment in Technology sub-categories — especially Copiers and Accessories. Consider bundling Office Supplies with Technology purchases to improve the basket value of those lower-margin items.
